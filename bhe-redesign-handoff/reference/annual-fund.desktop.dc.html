Questions

Why $650 per child?

The PTA invests about $930 per student each year. The suggested gift covers a large share of that, and families who can give more help close the gap for everyone.

Is my gift tax-deductible?

Yes. You’ll receive a receipt by email. Tax ID: [EIN].

Does this include PTA membership?

Yes — every Annual Fund gift includes membership for your family.
